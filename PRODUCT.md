## Users

Residential homeowners in Curitiba and region bringing family or antique furniture to be restored, reformed, painted, or revitalized; and commercial clients (restaurants, offices, stores) needing furniture renewal. ## Product Purpose

Binho Restauração de Móveis is a marcenaria in Curitiba (Água Verde) that restores, reforms, paints, and revitalizes residential and commercial furniture. Success means giving new life to old pieces — preserving their story and value — with reliable, quality work.
